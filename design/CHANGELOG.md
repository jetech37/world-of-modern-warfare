# World of Modern Warfare: Changelog

Everything built so far, newest first. World of Modern Warfare (WOMW) puts Modern Warfare 2's guns, movement and loadouts into World of Warcraft 1.12's world. It runs on a from-scratch WoW client (benilla) with MW2's assets layered on top, and a vmangos server with our own "fusion" rules.

---

## October 7, 2026

### Classes rebuilt: 3 core abilities and 6 picks
- **Every class has three archetypes:**
  - Warrior: Berserker, Vanguard, Warlord
  - Paladin: Crusader, Protector, Lightwarden
  - Hunter: Scout, Trapper, Slayer
  - Rogue: Assassin, Trickster, Bard
  - Priest: Shadow, Cleric, Oracle
  - Shaman: Elementalist, Earthbreaker, Totemist
  - Mage: Wizard, Spellsword, Artificer
  - Warlock: Afflicter, Felsworn, Necromancer
  - Druid: Shapeshifter, Grovekeeper, Starcaller
- **Small kits:**
  - Every character starts with **3 core abilities** and their class's out-of-combat utility spells.
  - At levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 you pick **one of three options**, one per archetype. Play pure or blend them.
  - The old spellbook is gone, and abilities grow with your level (no ranks to buy).
- **Class trainers sell the options** once each, for 1 to 60 gold by tier.
- **Hunters and warlocks no longer have pets.** Hunters are hunters without beasts; warlocks bind demons into themselves or raise the dead.
- **Archetype resources** show above the bar: Fury, Holy Power, Static and others.
- **Classes answer guns:** bullet-blocking shields and walls, smoke, spotting, temporary minions.
- About 105 of the 189 abilities work fully. The rest are partly in and still being finished.

### Companions are everyone's pet
- **Your battle companion now takes the real pet slot**, with the game's pet bar.
- **Aim it from first person:** Ctrl+1 on an enemy sends it in, on the ground sends it there, and a double tap brings it back. Its abilities aim where you look, and its health and cooldowns sit by the crosshair.
- **Choose its abilities:** each companion picks one of two abilities when it's first summoned and at each evolution. Autocast is smarter (taunts when you're shot, heals when you're low).
- **Rare traits:** companions drop with traits such as Ironhide, Swift, Venomous, Fire-touched, Scavenger and Gunner. Rarer drops carry more traits, and some pairs combine into a named bonus. Traits and rarity show on the item's tooltip, even at the auction house.
- **Breeding at stable masters:**
  - two adults of the same family make a new companion that can inherit traits;
  - each companion breeds at most three times;
  - lineage shows on the tooltip.
- **64 new companion lines** (spiders, bears, raptors, scorpids, undead, mechanicals, humanoids and more), each growing from pet-sized to mount-sized. Many more final forms can now be ridden.

### Kits and Quartermasters
- **Up to 5 kits:** your guns, attachments, camos, perks, equipment, killstreaks and your 6 class picks.
- The MW2 premade classes are gone.
- **Kits can only be edited or switched at a Quartermaster.** There are 75 of them now, each with its own name and look: at every inn and in the class trainer areas.
- **Quartermasters refill your ammo.** Every life starts with full reserves.
- **The Field Quartermaster's Ledger** (1 gold from any Quartermaster) lets you change kits anywhere, once an hour.
- **The kit editor** shows a "Combat stats" block and a **Talents** page.

### Downed, not dead
- At 0 health you go **down** instead of dying. You crawl, the screen reddens, and a bleed-out timer runs (more Stamina, more time).
- **With Last Stand you can fire your pistol** while down; without it you can only crawl.
- **Getting back up:**
  - Resurrection, Redemption, Rebirth and Ancestral Spirit, cast on a downed player;
  - a **Field Revival Kit** (First Aid);
  - a **Gnomish Revival Coil** (Engineering).

  Both kits also work on yourself while down, more slowly.
- When the timer runs out you die for real: ghost and corpse run as before.
- **No more durability loss or repair bills.**

### What gear does with guns
- **Gear never raises gun damage.** Instead:
  - Agility makes aiming, swapping and firing out of a sprint quicker.
  - Strength steadies recoil and lightens heavy guns.
  - Spirit brings health back sooner and faster (MW2-style regeneration).
  - Armor stops part of each bullet (up to about 30% in full plate).
  - Crit makes body shots count as weak-spot hits.
  - Intellect strengthens your grenades and flashes.
- **"Chance on hit" weapons and trinkets can trigger from gunfire.**
- Thresholds unlock small bonuses, e.g. 150 Agility: the first aim after a sprint is instant.

### The arsenal gets its own names
- **Every gun, attachment, camo, perk, killstreak and piece of equipment has a Warcraft name and an origin story.** A few examples:
  - the SPAS-12 is the **Bilgewater Scattercannon**
  - the Barrett is the **Thorium Siege Rifle**
  - the UAV is the **Gnomish Spy Gyro**
  - the Nuke is the **Gnomeregan Radiation Bomb**
- **Pick up guns** from dead NPC gunners and players: hold F. It's yours until you die.
- **Enchanted rounds** made by Enchanters: Incendiary, Frost, Armor-Piercing, Arcane and Holy. Each has a trade-off.
- **The Sentry Gun is now a killstreak** (5 kills), not something you build.

### A more dangerous world
- **Creatures are tougher and hit harder**, scaled by rank. Bosses keep their fights and just have more health.
- **Humanoid camps carry guns:** Defias, Scarlet Crusade, Dark Iron, Syndicate, Venture Co., pirates and more.

### Building
- **Anything built in the open world can be broken by guns and spells.**
  - Whoever breaks a piece salvages part of its materials.
  - Owners can reinforce pieces in three expensive tiers.
- **Bought homestead plots stay fully protected.**

### Homesteads in new places
- **Hyjal's plots were re-laid** on flat ground.
- **Two new homestead sites:** **Azshara Crater** and the **Thalassian plateau**, with couriers in Ironforge, the Undercity, Rut'theran, Thunder Bluff and Light's Hope.
- Falling out of a site or dying in one brings you back to its gate.

### Mounts
- **Shoot from the saddle.** Heavy hits and explosions can knock you off.
- **Glide** when you ride off a high ledge.
- **Updrafts:** take off from 11 launch points (city towers, the Dun Morogh airfield, the homestead gates) for a short flight.

### Prestige, battlegrounds and professions
- **Prestige any of three tracks:**
  - player level, at 60
  - gun unlocks
  - a maxed profession

  Each gives titles, tokens, camos and tracer colours. Massive rewards come when all three are maxed together. Look in the new **Prestige** tab of the achievements window (Y).
- **Battlegrounds score MW2 points**, and Tab shows the scoreboard.
- **Team Deathmatch** on the Gurubashi Arena floor: first to 30 kills.
- **Field gear from professions:**
  - Engineering: spare grenades
  - Leatherworking: bandoliers for more spare ammo
  - Tailoring: ghillie cloaks
  - Alchemy: combat stims

## October 6, 2026

### Hands: weapons and spells, two-hand style
- **V switches the action bar.** With the gun up, the main bar shows your loadout: primary, secondary, underbarrel, killstreak, lethal and tactical. With your hands up, it shows your own WoW abilities.
- **Abilities ready rather than fire.** With your hands up, pressing one:
  - casts a spell on yourself (a shout, a stance, a buff) at once;
  - queues a melee ability (Heroic Strike, Sinister Strike) for your next swing;
  - puts any other spell in your right hand (Shift for the left), and the mouse button casts it at whatever the crosshair is on.
- **What each hand holds:**
  - one-handers in the right fist, two-handers in both hands;
  - a shield on the left arm, which blocks with the right mouse button (only shields block);
  - a second weapon in the left hand, which swings with the right mouse button.
- **Spells glow** above the fist in their school's colour, and casting thrusts the hand forward.
- **The weapon draws up from below** when you raise your hands, and eases to the hip when you sprint.

### Real first-person animations
- **Your hands move with real animation tracks.** The first-person hands now play full animation tracks: the idles for one-handers, two-handed swords and axes, dual wield and fists, plus swings, drawing your weapon, the shield block, and casting with either hand.
- **Your arms follow them.** Your WoW arms and weapons are moved to match.

### Fingers
- **Fixed fingers that folded into flat planks and spikes,** in every first-person animation, guns included. Your character's thumb and fingers had been swapped, which also twisted the whole hand.
- **Hands round a weapon or shield** close the way your character's own model holds one.
- **Spell hands** take the tracks' finger poses, frame by frame, and the spell's glow sits in your palm.

### Battle companions and mounts
- **Companion items** are rare drops from creatures, critters included. They're Bind on Equip and keep their level when traded or sold.
- **A summoned companion fights beside you** in real combat, alongside any hunter or warlock pet.
- **Levels and evolution:**
  - companions level from 1 to 60, never above you;
  - they **evolve** into bigger forms, for example a whelpling into a whelp and then a drake, or a wolf pup into a dire wolf and then a war wolf;
  - evolving can need a catalyst, a certain place, or the right time of day.
- **Pet duels:** your companions fight each other while you stay out of it.
- **Mounts are companions too.** A companion's final form can be ridden: wolves, sabers, raptors, kodos, horses, mechanostriders, skeletal horses and rams. Getting off, or being dazed off, puts it back at your side mid-fight.

### Construction, woodcutting and homesteads
- **Construction is a secondary profession** (Apprentice to Artisan, 1–300). Every piece now costs materials and teaches skill. GMs still build for free.
- **Woodcutting:** lumber nodes in 12 forest zones; you need an axe to cut them.
- **Smithed parts:** nails, hinges, brackets and sentry parts are crafted at the trainer.
- **Homesteads:** plots for sale in three sizes on Hyjal's never-opened summit.
  - A courier in Stormwind and Orgrimmar takes you there and back.
  - A steward sells plots, takes upkeep and grants permits.
  - **Bought plots are completely safe:** only you and the people you permit can build, open doors, or take anything down, and raids never reach them.
- **Creatures walk around your walls** when there's a way, rather than always smashing through.

### Third person
- **F5 switches between first and third person**, and guns, melee and spells all work in both.
- **Your character holds its MW2 gun.** Shots leave from your eyes toward the crosshair.
- **Your body plays the animation:** firing, each weapon's swing, casting, blocking and drawing your weapon.

### Unlocks through achievements
- **New characters start with only the USP .45.** Everything else is earned through **598 achievements**: every gun, attachment, camo, perk, deathstreak, piece of equipment and killstreak.
- **Each unlock has a path that fits it.** For example, the UMP45 comes from killing humanoids with pistols, the SPAS-12 from beasts at close range, and the L86 from elites.
- **Y opens the achievements window:** tabs, progress bars, and what each achievement unlocks.
- **Earned achievements** pop up on screen and are announced in chat.
- **Locked choices in Create-a-Class** are marked in red, with the achievement that unlocks them.
- **Unlocks are shared across your account.** GMs have everything.

### Create-a-Class and guns
- **Quartermasters** stand beside every innkeeper (45 inns, every capital included). Talking to one opens Create-a-Class, and classes can only be edited there; switching between saved classes still works anywhere.
- **Any three perks**, from any tier.
- **Killstreaks are PvP only.** A kill is worth one streak point against a player your level or higher, 0.1 less per level below you, never under 0.1.
- **Every perk does something in the classic world too:**
  - Scavenger: +25% gold from creatures.
  - Sleight of Hand: faster spellcasting.
  - Scrambler: slows creatures' spells aimed at you.
  - Stopping Power: harder melee hits.
  - Lightweight: half fall damage.
  - Hardline: +10% kill XP.
  - Cold-Blooded: creatures don't call their friends on you.
  - Ninja: harder to spot in stealth.
  - SitRep: spots stealth from farther.
  - Marathon: faster out of combat.
- **Equipment:**
  - grenades, claymores and C4 show their MW2 models;
  - smoke grenades leave a drifting cloud;
  - a flashbang whites out the screen of anyone it catches, and a stun grenade shakes their view.

### Sharing
- **The first friends' release is up,** and the installer can be downloaded from this site. The invite code is typed once on the first run.

## October 5, 2026

### Sharing with friends
- **Hosted server.** The game now runs on a Linux VPS, so the host's PC doesn't have to be on. It covers login, world and sign-up, with HTTPS, and every service restarts itself after a crash.
- **One-click installer, `WorldOfModernWarfare.exe`.** On first run it installs itself and adds a desktop shortcut. It asks for the invite code once, makes your account, and downloads the game.
- **Automatic updates.** Every start downloads only the files that changed. Downloads resume if interrupted, every file is hash-checked, and the launcher updates itself.
- **Sign-up is protected.** It needs the invite code, is rate-limited, and can never overwrite an existing account.
- **This site.** It shows live server status (online, players, uptime, version), an "Updating" notice while a big release uploads, release notes, and the installer download.
- **The new name: World of Modern Warfare (WOMW).**

### Melee
- The main-hand weapon is held in the **right fist**; two-handers are held in both hands. Before, it sat in the left hand from MW2's knife animation.
- **Fixed the upside-down sword.** The blade is lined up from each weapon model's own shape.
- The arms reach for the weapon through every swing. Light swings alternate right-to-left and left-to-right, and sprinting carries the weapon low.
- Melee is now a weapon you hold: **V** raises it and the trigger swings it, like the riot shield. Lunges only work while standing.

### Weapons
- **Grenade launchers** fire a real arc, carry about 135 yards, and burst where the round lands.
- **The RPG** shows its rocket in the tube, and reloads load a fresh one.
- **Akimbo:** each gun keeps its own magazine, the left mouse button fires the left gun, and both guns are put away on a swap.
- **Shotguns** load shell by shell. The ammo count climbs as each shell goes in, and you can interrupt the reload with a partial load.
- Reloads cancelled by a swap no longer fill the magazine.
- The underbarrel launcher keeps the rifle's own look; no stray sights from other guns.
- **Bling Pro** allows a second attachment on the secondary.
- **The heartbeat sensor** shows enemies on the sensor's own screen, bright enough to read. It no longer jumps onto your secondary during a swap.

### Movement
- **Mantling** works onto built pieces and from anywhere in a jump's rise. You see and hear the vault: the gun dips and the gear rattles.
- The crouched view is steadier.

### Enemies
- **NPC gunners** carry MW2 guns in their hands and you see them fire: muzzle flash, tracer and the gun's sound.
- **They fight like MW2's campaign and Spec Ops soldiers.** They:
  - hold their ground at their gun's range;
  - strafe, take cover and peek;
  - move as squads;
  - get more accurate the longer they're on you;
  - lob frag grenades.

### Building
- **Valheim-style building.** Floors, walls, stairs, doors (opened with F), sandbags, blueprints that go up whole, and WoW props as pieces.
- WoW props and other ground pieces can stand on built floors.
- **Sentry guns** that turn, spin up and fire like MW2's.
- Bedrolls, and raids by fighters.
- Debris and sounds when pieces break.
- `.fusion clear` takes down a test site (GM).

### Other
- **Killcam.**
- Muzzle flashes; rockets and explosions with real blasts.
- Aiming down sights fixed for all guns; rounds leave from the muzzle.

### Behind the scenes
- **One repository** for the client, the WoW client fork and the server.
- **Windows package with no Docker.** It has Play, Start Server and Stop Server, its own database, and the game files bundled.
- The server builds with Visual C++ on Windows and as a Linux build for the VPS.

## October 4, 2026

### Create-a-Class
- **Create-a-Class window.** It covers primary and secondary weapons, attachments, camos, perks and deathstreaks, with MW2's own icons, and classes can be renamed.
- Classes are saved on the server and checked against MW2's rules. The equipped class stays after login.
- Swap guns with 1 and 2 or the mouse wheel, using MW2's own swap animations.

### Guns
- **Real MW2 numbers** for every weapon, taken from MW2's own weapon files: damage, magazine, fire rate, reload times and spread.
- **Attachments change the gun**, using MW2's numbers.
- **Sights:**
  - see-through red dots and holographic sights with MW2's reticles;
  - sniper scopes that aim through MW2's overlay, with sway and hold breath;
  - **thermal sights**: bodies glow white-hot, with outlines.
- **Camos** show on the gun.
- **Shotgun pellets and spread**, with hits checked on the server.
- Riot shield, FMJ, and splash damage numbers.
- **Underbarrel weapons** (grenade launcher and shotgun) and **akimbo**.

### Perks and streaks
- **Perks in play**, for example Hardline on the HUD and Sleight of Hand reloads. Stopping Power doesn't apply to explosives.
- **Equipment and killstreak call-ins**, with streaks counted on the server.

### Combat and world
- **Your own arms in first person.** Every race's arms fit the grip, lit by WoW's light.
- Rounds fired at you show a tracer from the shooter, with its sound.
- **Creatures hear gunfire**, call for help, and chase further.
- **NPCs with guns.**
- A minimap radar.

### Movement
- **MW2 stances.** Correct eye heights and transitions; hold C to go prone; Space to rise.
- **Prone:** turning is slowed past 60°.
- **Sprint** has a stamina bar and MW2's sprint animations, and you can't fire for a moment after.
- **MW2's sprint and prone speeds**, view bob per stance, and a landing dip.
- **Mantle** and **knife lunge**.
- MW2's gun and movement sounds.

## October 3, 2026

### The start
- **The MW2 layer on top of a WoW 1.12 client.** It reads MW2's guns, animations and sounds straight from your MW2 install.
- **Holding MW2's own gun** in first person: one gun, in the hand, solid and aimed.
- **Azeroth Warfare mode:** real rounds, stances, the Armory, and a quartermaster with one-click rearm.
- **The server's fusion rules.** Shooting, stances, sprint, mantle, lunge and perks are checked on the server, with a full test suite.
- Creatures close in on distant shooters.
- A load-test bot and a server kit for setting it all up.
