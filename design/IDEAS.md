# Ideas: promising, not yet built

A running list. Move an item into GAME_DESIGN.md when it is agreed, and off this list when it ships.

## Systems
- **Spellcasting, next** (the base is in GAME_DESIGN.md, Spellcasting): mixed pairs in the two
  hands make hybrids; a bolt released at nothing flies on and hits what it meets; channels held
  on the button (Arcane Missiles, Drain Life); more ground spells (Chain Lightning arcing from the
  point, Hurricane and Blizzard at the crosshair, Prayer of Healing as a circle).
- **Druid forms done right**: no human hands; the creature's own forelimbs in first person, shown
  only on attack or cast (worgen keeps clawed hands); every form its own action bar.
- **Abilities tab** in the kit window (spellbook replacement).
- **Construction blocks** (Enshrouded-like hybrid): grid cubes, wedges, stairs, arches in WoW's
  own textures with auto edge blending; prefabs and saved blueprints alongside.
- **Homestead stations** that work: forge, alchemy, gunsmith bench, storage, player shop stall.
- **Farming and ranching**: crops on real time with seasons and watering; pens of companion
  species that produce materials.
- **Companion jobs**: spare companions gather, guard or fish at the homestead while you're away.
- **Gunsmithing**: Engineering crafts guns and attachments; boss blueprints; named, signed weapons.
- **Weather and night in combat**: rain and snow add spread at range, night favours thermal and
  night vision, fog favours melee and stealth.

## Content
- **Extraction runs**: instances as drop-in, loot, get out alive to keep it.
- **Frontline zones**: contested zones with buildable, capturable faction bases.
- **Contracts board**: daily bounties, escorts, homestead defence, rare-creature hunts.
- **Legendary creatures**: rare roaming elites, unique companion species, server-wide alerts.

## Social and scale
- **Proximity voice chat**: spatial, whisper/talk/yell ranges, radios and megaphones as items,
  muffled through walls, calls for help while downed. Mumble Link first, built-in later.
- **Bots**: party bots and hireable mercenaries for solo play; battle bots that fill frontlines
  and battlegrounds when few are online. Always shown as bots. The wanderers (WANDERERS.md, Phase
  1 built) are the start: a known wanderer promoted to a party member is the mercenary.
- **Solo to hundreds**: instances and world events scale to who is there; companions and
  mercenaries fill a solo player's party.

## AI and combat tuning
- NPC gun accuracy: an aim cone that tightens while held and widens with range, movement and cover.
- Gunshots alert mobs by hearing range (about 60 yd, 15 suppressed); hit mobs call for help;
  aggroed mobs rush at about 1.6x speed.
- Player guns: longer range, MW2 damage falloff, less hip spread.

## Identity and world
- **The Rift story**: modern weapons arrive in Azeroth through a rift; seasons are story arcs that
  change the world for good (a zone overrun, a city rebuilt by players).
- **Living player towns**: homestead districts that grow into towns NPCs move into, with shops,
  guards and quests, and fall back if abandoned.
- **Player radio**: a broadcast station item; one player talks or plays to every tuned radio.
- **Vehicles**: craftable gnomish tanks, goblin buggies, gyrocopters; crewed, repairable, destructible.
- **Sieges**: destructible walls and gates in frontline bases and player towns.
- **Roles beyond combat**: medics, engineers, smugglers running contraband between zones, bounty hunters.
- **Hunting**: tracking wildlife by sign and sound, scopes and calls; trophies and companion essences.
- **Hardcore realm**: optional permadeath season.
- **Clips**: killcam and replay export to share.
