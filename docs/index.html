How to play

  1. Download the installer below and run it. It installs itself and adds a desktop shortcut.
  2. Create your free account: pick an account name and password in the launcher (or later with Create Account on the game's login screen). Already have an account? Just log in.
  3. The first run downloads the game (about 16 GB). After that, each start fetches only what changed.
  4. Log in with your account in the game. The launcher always puts you on the latest version.
  5. New here? The game opens a short guide the first time you play; type /help to see it again.
You must own and have legal access to World of Warcraft and the original Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2009) to play.

Features

Very early alpha: expect bugs, missing pieces and big changes.

  • First-person shooter combat in a classic MMO world
  • A full modern arsenal: weapons, attachments, camos, equipment and perks, all with lore-friendly names
  • Everything unlocked through in-game achievements that reward playing every way
  • First-person melee, and spells cast from your hands at the crosshair
  • Build destructible structures in the open world, or rent a protected plot in unfinished zones
  • Collect companions that fight at your side, level up, learn moves and evolve, some into mounts, and trade them
  • Downed and revive: no one gets left behind
